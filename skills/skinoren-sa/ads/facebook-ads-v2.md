## Notes on structural choices

- **Hooks** are 20 words or less and anchor on a specific, visualisable scene (wedding photographer, Hinge profile, interview bathroom, pill-shadow face, baby photos). Every hook is a daily moment that Thandi would recognise.
- **Mechanism** is woven in through a trusted-authority figure (pharmacist, dermatologist, nurse) rather than stated as marketing claim.
- **Hidden problem** across all 5 ads: cosmetic-grade is tested on lighter skin and dosed below clinical levels. This explains why her previous attempts failed, which is Schwartz Level 3-4 copy.
- **Price anchor** stays at R350 (matches Clicks price so no "too cheap, must be fake" trigger).
- **Scarcity** uses the real nationwide stock shortage ("Clicks out of stock," "Dis-Chem haven't had it in months") rather than fake urgency.
- **Safety reversal** in ads 4 and 5 directly counters the hydroquinone objection by naming ochronosis and the pregnancy-safe mechanism.
- **No em dashes**, no marketing speak, no quoted forum blocks. Every ad lands at the same soft close: your skin, your face, your life, with permission to show up again.
